# Bootstrap Contract

This public plugin must remain small and safe to publish.

## Responsibilities

- Load through Codex Git marketplace.
- Point Codex to a locally synced SharePoint `AI Agent` folder.
- Preserve AgeVolt's explicit-edit gate for company modules and rules.
- Require revision history updates for explicit company changes.

## Non-Responsibilities

- Do not store internal KB.
- Do not mirror SharePoint data.
- Do not include credentials or customer data.
- Do not replace the internal SharePoint source of truth.
